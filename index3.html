About Pine Economics

I help sustainable transport projects find their economic case. Excellent interventions — bus priority, bike lanes, walkable neighbourhoods — often struggle to win support because they lack the analytical frameworks that car-based transport has enjoyed for decades.

I work with cities, consultancies, and transport authorities to build robust business cases using rigorous economic analysis and clear communication that resonates with decision-makers and the public.

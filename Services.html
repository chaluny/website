Services

Troubleshooting: Business Case & Assurance

Has your business case failed to gain traction? This service is designed to get your project back on track. I specialise in rapidly diagnosing flaws in business cases, identifying gaps in evidence, and implementing a clear plan to achieve a successful submission. It’s not just about finding problems; it’s about fixing them.
  • What I do: A forensic review of all five cases (Strategic, Economic, Financial, Commercial, Management), stress-testing of models and assumptions, and hands-on leadership to rebuild the analysis.
  • When you need it: After a failed audit, a rejection from an investment board, or when you know your current business case won't withstand scrutiny.

Research: Strategic Analysis & Evidence Base Development

Every successful strategy is built on a foundation of robust evidence. We provide the deep analytical work needed to explore problems, test solutions, and build an unshakeable evidence base for your proposals. From sophisticated cost forecasting to developing new policy frameworks, we deliver the insights you need to make informed decisions.

  • What I do: Econometric analysis, demand forecasting, policy framework development, international benchmarking, and cost-effectiveness analysis.
  • When you need it: When you're developing a new policy, exploring the feasibility of a major investment, or need a stronger evidence base to support your strategic objectives.

Narratives: Strategic Case & Storytelling

A technically perfect economic case can still fail if it doesn't tell a clear and compelling story. I specialise in crafting evidence-based narratives that win over stakeholders, from public officials and politicians to the public. I bridge the gap between technical analysis and strategic communication, ensuring your project is understood and supported.

  • What I do: Authoring Strategic Cases, translating complex economic benefits for non-technical audiences, and aligning the business case with wider policy goals.
  • When you need it: When you need to build political and public support for a project, or when you need to articulate how your scheme delivers more than just a positive Benefit-Cost Ratio.
